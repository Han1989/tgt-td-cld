Privacy

Tower Defense Together · last updated 3 October 2026

Tower Defense Together is a free browser game made by an independent developer in Singapore. There are no accounts. The game sends a small amount of anonymous play data to its own server so we can tell whether new players enjoy it and come back. This page says what, why, for how long, and how to turn it off.

The short version

  • No accounts: we never ask for your name, email or password.
  • No ads, no third-party trackers or analytics services, and the game sets no cookies.
  • Play data is deleted after 30 days.
  • You can turn it off for this browser at any time (below, or in the game: ⚙ Settings → Play data).
  • We never sell it or pass it on. Only the game's own server stores it.

What the game sends, and why

A random visitor id
Made by your browser and kept in its storage (tdt.visitor). It is not tied to your name, email or device. We use it to count players who come back the next day or the next week.
Visits and playtime
A new id for each visit, when it starts and ends, and a heartbeat every 25 seconds while the game is on screen.
Where the visit came from
The tag on the link you followed (for example ?src=reddit-playmygame). Without a tag, only whether you came from CrazyGames, another site, or nowhere. Never the full address of the page you came from.
Platform
Web, iOS or Android, worked out in your browser. Nothing more about your device is kept.
Match results
Won or lost, the Heart's HP left, Full or Quick, the wave reached, and how many players. This is how we tune difficulty.
Ratings and notes, only if you leave one
A 1–5 rating at the end of a match and an optional note of up to 140 characters. Please don't put personal details in a note.

We don't collect your name, email, contacts, location or advertising ids, and we don't store your IP address with any of this. The data is pseudonymous: a random id, not you.

Playing online

The nickname you type is shown to the others in your room. When an online match ends, the game server writes one log line with the room code, each player's nickname and hero, and the match numbers. Pick a nickname that isn't your real name. Save match report stays on your device unless you send it to someone.

Who hosts it, and where

Vercel serves the game's files and Render runs the game server, in Singapore. Like any web host, they keep their own request logs, which include IP addresses, under their own policies. We use no other services. If you play from outside Singapore, your play data is stored in Singapore.

The legal basis

Under the EU and UK GDPR we rely on our legitimate interest in finding out whether the game works and is fun; you can object at any time with the switch below. Under Singapore's PDPA, this page is our notice of the purpose, we use the data only for it, and you can withdraw by turning it off.

How long we keep it

Play data is deleted automatically 30 days after it was sent. The visitor id stays in your browser until you clear this site's data.

Your choices

Send anonymous play data from this browser

The switch needs JavaScript. Without it, clear this site's data in your browser to remove the id.

  • Off stops every event from this browser straight away, ratings included. What was already sent is still deleted after 30 days, or sooner if you ask.
  • If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, it starts off. You can still turn it on.
  • The same switch is in the game: ⚙ Settings → Play data.

Children

There are no accounts, chat is a few fixed phrases, and we never ask for personal details. If a child has typed personal details into a note, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If any of this changes, we will update this page and its date. Accounts, if they come later, will get a fuller policy before they launch.
