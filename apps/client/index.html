      Controls layout

      Stick

      Skills

      Floating: drag anywhere on the map to walk, and the stick appears under your thumb. Fixed: the stick stays where it is drawn. Hold any skill button to read every skill.

      Joystick

      Left, center or right. The skill buttons move with the stick.

      Stick feel

      Light walks at full speed from a short drag. Firm asks for a longer push. Normal is in between.

      Graphics

      Screen shake

      Shakes on ultimates, boss abilities and Heart hits. Low graphics and your device's reduced-motion setting turn it off.

      Display

      Bright lifts the ground and shadows for playing outdoors.

      Play data

      It is playtime, platform, match results and ratings, kept 30 days. No accounts, no trackers. Privacy

      Your data

      A copy of what the game server holds for this browser, or delete it. Delete also gives this browser a new id.
