      Controls
      • Alt + Left-click ping the map · C quick chat (no typing)
      • Right-click move / attack
      • A + Left-click attack-move
      • Q W E R skills (targeted ones: then left-click) · E is passive
      • Shift + Q/W/E/R or + learn a skill · R from level 6
      • Left-click a pad to build, your tower to upgrade / sell / set its target priority
      • B then 1–5 build · U upgrade the selected tower
      • S stop · Esc cancel
      • Camera: screen edges, arrows, middle-drag, wheel zoom, Space centres on hero
